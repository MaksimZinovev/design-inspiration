# Dot-Matrix Agent Status Display

## Overall
A dark (near-black) rounded-square smart display panel (like a Tidbyt / pixel-matrix device) mounted on a warm beige wall with soft ambient light and subtle shadow — realistic 3D mockup with slight thickness visible on the right edge.

## Content (top → bottom)
1. **Header** — small white pixel/mono caps: "01 AGENT STATUS", with "01" boxed/outlined.
2. **Headline** — large dot-matrix text: "THINKING..." in white pixel font.
3. **Face** — centered circle of white dots (LED-matrix style) with two glowing orange-red eyes and a small dot mouth — cute robot expression.
4. **Waveform** — horizontal dotted audio waveform: white dots flanking, vivid orange-red dots in the center (voice activity).
5. **Footer stats** — pixel-font stats with small gray caps labels over white values, separated by thin vertical dividers: "STEPS 128 | TOOLS 12 | MEMORY 98%".

## Styling
- Palette: monochrome white/gray dots on true black (#000–#111); accent vivid orange-red (~#FF4A00) for eyes + waveform center; warm wall background (~#C8B49A).
- Typography: pixel / dot-matrix font (e.g. Departure Mono, or LED-dot style).
- Shape: rounded corners ~24–32px, matte black, soft gradient lighting, subtle drop shadow for depth.

## Notes
- Name: dot-matrix-histogram (the dotted waveform row reads like a histogram).
- Fully replicable as a canvas design.
